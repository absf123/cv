Deok-Joong Lee

Ph.D. Candidate · Artificial Intelligence

Hello. I’m Deok-Joong Lee, a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University.

My research focuses on geometric deep learning for protein science, particularly protein–protein interaction modeling, protein docking, and protein design. I also work on drug discovery and biomedical AI, with broader interests in optimization methods and brain imaging.

My long-term goal is to develop geometric and generative AI models that can understand, predict, and design biomolecules, ultimately contributing to protein engineering and therapeutic discovery.

Research Interests

My research focuses on graph neural networks, optimization, and generative modeling across biomedical and molecular domains. In particular, I am interested in modeling protein interactions and protein structures.
